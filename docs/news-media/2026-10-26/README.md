# October 26, 2026 editorial release

Five independently researched stories, each with complete Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Russian and French editions. The batch adds 25 article records to the existing 450; old article paths, text, publication timestamps, trust/contact pages and SEO/GEO implementation remain intact.

## Auditable source and media records

- `source-use-audit.json` records all 30 references, their date and rights basis, 13 cumulative restricted-source budgets across the five editions, and hashes of the final 25 manuscripts. Open-licensed and government-authored material is distinguished from protected issuer/press text; hosting on an official domain is not treated as an automatic licence.
- `provenance.json` records six master assets, 48 responsive derivatives, visible five-language labels, exact hashes and eight source-photo decisions. Four conceptual illustrations are explicitly AI-labelled, one official statistical graphic is reproduced unchanged in the body only, and one licensed archival portrait is identified as archival rather than election-night photography.
- `official-result-snapshots.json` preserves the timestamps and denominators of the election pages used. These are attributed snapshots, not claims that every later legal procedure is finished.

## Selection and preservation

The eight briefing candidates were reviewed, and five were selected for substantive, nonduplicative reporting. The Claude item overlapped an earlier incident article and lacked sufficient independently verified new detail for another full report; the iron-ore commentary lacked sufficiently verified underlying evidence for a new full analysis; the GM item did not establish a fresh event for this edition. These three were not padded into extra articles.

Only this issue’s content/media/audit files, one publication-log append, an image-specific focal-position rule and a single-photo delivery-quality override for the new portrait are changed. The latter preserves the existing cover geometry and leaves all previous selectors untouched. Withdrawn articles remain withdrawn.

New articles carry the issue date only until an exact first-live publication instant can be evidenced. No prepublication, future or invented RSS timestamp is inserted. Production completion requires the exact Git commit to reach native Vercel READY and the public domain to serve that commit, followed by page, image, feed, sitemap, old-route and responsive-browser checks; the commit alone is not completion.
