## Source-use method

The starting material was five **complete supplied manuscripts**, including their analysis and eight original source notes. Adapting that supplied text is recorded separately from introducing quotations, translations or new summaries of externally retrieved source expression. Fact-checking the linked pages does not by itself turn every inherited paragraph into newly reproduced external prose. The supplied source titles are retained as bibliographic identifiers.

No newly retrieved source-body passage was copied or translated into these editions. No new external narrative or replacement article was introduced. The OpenAI changes remove an unsupported chronology and clarify the final usage ratio already present in the supplied manuscript. The Arizona wording removes an unsupported interpretation rather than adding a new technical claim. The rest of the editorial changes concern readability, localization, headings, attribution and source-note formatting.

For added material from a newly consulted page, this review applies a **source-specific ceiling of 200 words across all five languages combined**, not a separate allowance for each edition, and a separate ceiling of 25 newly quoted words per source. These are ceilings for new external expression, not quotas, and are not charged against the supplied manuscripts or their original analysis. If an external passage is added later, its cumulative use must be reassessed against that source's applicable limit.

## Boundaries

This is an editorial source-handling review, not a claim that a publicly accessible company page grants general republication rights. The absence of a general page licence is not a blanket reason to reject transformation of the supplied manuscripts. Equally, text review does not grant rights to company photographs, diagrams or videos. Source-original illustrations must not be substituted for the separately selected and licensed archival photographs without their own rights check.

No full source article, newly translated source passage or source-original company illustration was added during this text review. Claims requiring independent operational or product-performance evidence remain qualified rather than presented as verified outcomes.
