Relevant full-report methods were checked; the entire 116-page report was not read page by page.

4. ADP: the original Employe.jpg is a standing indoor portrait, not a photograph of office work. Use the existing November 4 job-fair asset instead, with its unchanged multilingual historical caption and attribution.

5. Vaccine: do not use the 2014 injection image on a card without its immediately adjacent unrelated-trial disclaimer. The selected replacement is a CDC public-domain historical Ebola-virus micrograph. It must not be called a Bundibugyo sample, a vaccine image, or an image from Tumaini.
