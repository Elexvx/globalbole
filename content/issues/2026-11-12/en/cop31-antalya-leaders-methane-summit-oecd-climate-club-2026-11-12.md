On November 12, 2026, [day two of the Antalya Leaders' Summit at the UN Climate Change Conference (COP31) opened at 09:00 with a high-level Methane Summit](https://unfccc.int/cop31), bringing world leaders together to accelerate practical reductions in methane and other non-CO2 greenhouse gases to curb the magnitude and duration of near-term 1.5°C temperature overshoot[^2]. According to [the official UNFCCC High-Level Segment programme](https://unfccc.int/zh/node/662632), the November 12 summit schedule also features two flagship leadership sessions: 'High-Level Roundtable: The Second Decade of the Paris Agreement: Accelerating Implementation and Access to Finance' (12:00–13:30) and 'High-Level Dialogue: Urban Resilience, Adaptation and Climate Action' (13:30–17:00)[^1]. Simultaneously in Paris, [the OECD published its new policy brief titled Climate Club guidance on country platforms for industry transformation](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/forthcoming.html), establishing an operational framework to mobilize public and private capital for heavy-industry decarbonization[^3].

## COP31 Methane Summit and Second-Decade Paris Agreement Finance Roundtables in Antalya

Opened on November 11 by [Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan and UN Secretary-General António Guterres](https://unfccc.int/zh/node/662632), the first part of the COP31 High-Level Segment gathers Heads of State and Government in Antalya on November 11 and 12 (with a resumed session scheduled for November 17–18)[^1]. At the November 12 Methane Summit, [UNFCCC officials underscored that with atmospheric methane concentrations nearly triple pre-industrial levels and strong El Niño conditions intensifying near-term warming, rapid abatement of methane emissions across oil and gas operations, coal mines, agriculture, and waste systems is indispensable to preventing irreversible climate damages](https://unfccc.int/cop31)[^2].

During the afternoon High-Level Segment sessions on November 12, [national leaders addressed the twin implementation bottlenecks of climate finance access and urban climate adaptation](https://unfccc.int/zh/node/662632)[^1]. Addressing heads of state in Antalya, UNFCCC Executive Secretary Simon Stiell warned that 'the political costs of fossil fuel dependency are unforgiving' and urged governments to use the second decade of the Paris Agreement to lower capital barriers for clean energy and grid modernization in developing economies, while scaling urban resilience against intensifying heatwaves and floods[^1][^2].

## OECD and Climate Club Unveil Country Platform Blueprint for Heavy-Industry Transformation

Complementing the COP31 finance deliberations, [the OECD released its November 12 policy brief providing Climate Club guidance on country platforms for industry transformation](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/forthcoming.html)[^3]. Developed within the intergovernmental Climate Club—supported by the OECD and the International Energy Agency (IEA)—the guidance addresses the financing gap facing hard-to-abate industrial sectors such as steel, cement, and chemicals in emerging and developing economies[^3].

Rather than relying on fragmented project-by-project transactions, the OECD guidance outlines how host governments can establish country-led coordination platforms that align national industrial decarbonization roadmaps, carbon pricing and green public procurement standards, concessional blended finance from multilateral development banks, and private off-take commitments[^3]. Together, the COP31 Methane Summit and the OECD Climate Club country-platform blueprint reflect a dual track in late-2026 climate policy: pairing immediate reductions in short-lived super-pollutants with institutional financing mechanisms to decarbonize heavy manufacturing[^1][^2][^3].
