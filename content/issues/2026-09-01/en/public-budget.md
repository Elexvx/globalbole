## Key observations

Publishing a spreadsheet is only the beginning. Readers also need to understand the service behind each allocation, who owns the project and how progress can be checked.

## How to verify

Connecting budgets, contracts and delivery reports helps the public compare promises with outcomes. Explaining measurement methods and retaining revision histories are equally important.
