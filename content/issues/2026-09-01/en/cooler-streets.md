## Key observations

A walkable street needs more than a smooth pavement. Shade, seating, drinking water and clear signs shape the daily experience of older residents, children and commuters.

## How to verify

Assessments should combine observations at different times with residents’ feedback. Maintenance costs belong in the plan so that new facilities remain useful after construction.
