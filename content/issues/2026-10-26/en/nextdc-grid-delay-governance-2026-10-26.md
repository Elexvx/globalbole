On October 26, NEXTDC announced its CEO’s FY27 incentive waiver, incumbent non-executive directors’ 10% fee cut and chair succession.[^1] For data-center investors, management accountability is only one layer of the problem. Another is aligning capital spending, local infrastructure and services customers can actually use on the same timetable.

## A completed building is not a deliverable service

M3’s power delay removed about A$140 million each of forecast net revenue and underlying EBITDA; construction continues, while customer contractual rights affect actual contributions.[^2]

This contrast shows why data-center progress cannot be measured solely by how much of a building is complete. A structure with equipment space, an electricity connection capable of handling additional demand and a computing service that can meet its ongoing obligations are interdependent but distinct things. If one is not ready, another’s percentage completion cannot simply stand in for it.

Victoria’s Sustainable Data Centre Action Plan, published in September, puts these dependencies within a single framework. Site selection, electricity supply and grid readiness, water use, local employment and community benefits all require coordination. They cannot be treated as incidental matters to resolve after the building work is finished.[^3]

## Buying energy and connecting to the grid are different tasks

The action plan calls for additional electricity demand to be supported by new renewable-energy and storage investment. Projects must also cover connection and network-expansion costs caused by their additional demand, including necessary upstream upgrades. Some implementation and verification details still require coordination with national standards; not every initiative in the plan represents an already completed approval system.[^3]

The physical connection is easily overlooked. Buying generation or signing a power-purchase agreement addresses energy sources and commercial arrangements; it does not automatically deliver substations, line capacity or connection works on time. Even when an energy-supply contract is signed early, the particular site may still require a separate set of engineering and permission processes. Generation, transmission and distribution, and the data-center building cannot each promise an appealing completion date that fails to connect with the others.

Assessing delivery capacity therefore requires more than asking whether customers and funding exist. It also means asking whether customers’ required load profiles correspond to a supply route with established responsibilities, construction prerequisites and acceptance milestones.

## Community relations are not a “soft issue” outside engineering

The state action plan incorporates road access for construction, traffic management, noise, backup generation and community engagement into development. It emphasizes proactive engagement with local communities and responsible authorities even when a project follows an accelerated approval route.[^3]

This does not mean every objection should block construction indefinitely. It means infrastructure uses actual roads, land and public resources. If operators identify these effects only at the last stage, the cost may emerge as route changes, revised construction sequencing or later connection. Early engagement is valuable not only for securing support, but also for discovering constraints missing from the original plan.

Environmental oversight is likewise not a single blanket permit. Victoria’s EPA distinguishes planning decisions from environmental regulation: it does not decide where data centers are located. Some facilities may need environmental permissions because of particular activities, but all operators have a general environmental duty to control pollution and waste risks. Noise, air pollution and chemical storage are among the risks that must be identified.[^4]

## New policy cannot retroactively pass judgment on an older project

M3 application PA2604231 was received on February 17, 2026.[^5] Signed in October, the statement covers applications lodged from September 23, excluding earlier filings and related amendments.[^6]

The new framework can therefore help explain the industry’s future regulatory direction. It cannot establish that M3 violated the new standards or that an approval delay resulted directly from a requirement introduced later. Project accountability must be traced to the specific application, applicable rules and parties’ contracts, rather than inferred from an industry-policy headline.

This distinction also matters to investment judgments. Clearer regulatory direction may help future projects arrange resources earlier. Yet clarity does not eliminate costs, nor does it mean work already underway will immediately gain access to electricity.

## Governance changes must ultimately produce verifiable milestones

Even addressing investors’ concerns about accountability cannot directly create a megawatt of usable electricity. Operationally meaningful improvements should identify who owns cross-functional dependencies, which milestones remain unresolved, how commitments change when delays occur and when the market will receive updates it can verify.

For data-center companies, the most useful disclosure may not be a larger distant capacity figure, but more robust near-term delivery conditions. Can power works be matched to customer-service arrangements? Are construction and environmental responsibilities clear? Is unfinished government coordination accurately reflected? The state action plan’s emphasis on interagency coordination and transparency over infrastructure progress also aims to reduce situations in which each party holds only part of the information, yet all ultimately bear the delay.[^3]

What warrants continued attention is not whether a pay adjustment quickly lifts market sentiment, but whether the operating timetable moves from “expected to be possible” to commitments with named owners, prerequisites and individually verifiable milestones. Only by treating the building, grid, community and customers as one delivery process can capital investment have a chance to become reliable service.
