At its [regular press conference on November 12](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/xwfbzt/2026/swbzklxxwfbh2026n11y12r/index.html), China's Ministry of Commerce (MOFCOM) spokesperson He Yadong detailed exhibition outcomes from the 9th China International Import Expo (CIIE) in Shanghai, highlighted more than 100 cooperation intentions signed during a dedicated China-EU medical device matchmaking session, objected to 5G vendor restrictions in Germany and Portugal, and confirmed active bilateral communication on a reciprocal '$30 billion for $30 billion' tariff reduction framework with the United States[^1]. On the same day, China's National Development and Reform Commission (NDRC) issued a [domestic refined fuel price adjustment notice](https://www.ndrc.gov.cn/xwdt/xwfb/202611/t20261112_1408795.html) cutting benchmark gasoline and diesel ceilings by RMB 645 and RMB 625 per metric ton effective midnight on November 12[^2].

## 9th CIIE Surpasses 370,000 Square Meters as China-EU MedTech Matchmaking Yields Over 100 Agreements

According to the [official MOFCOM briefing transcript](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/xwfbzt/2026/swbzklxxwfbh2026n11y12r/index.html), the corporate exhibition hall at the 9th CIIE exceeded 370,000 square meters, while 69 countries and international organizations participated in the country exhibition and the Hongqiao International Economic Forum released dual flagship reports for the first time[^1]. Organizers also inaugurated a global heritage brand showcase featuring roughly 100 time-honored consumer brands from guest-of-honor and theme countries—including 30 century-old brands—and confirmed that preparatory work for the 10th CIIE is already underway[^1].

During the expo on November 5, MOFCOM joined the Ministry of Finance and the National Health Commission to host a specialized China-EU medical technology procurement matchmaking event implementing consensus from the second session of the China-EU Trade and Investment Consultation Mechanism[^1]. More than 160 Chinese medical institutions and enterprises engaged directly with over 40 European medical device manufacturers, reaching more than 100 cooperation intentions benefiting companies from Germany, Ireland, the Netherlands, and Sweden[^1]. Because cooperation intentions represent commercial matchmaking commitments rather than settled customs deliveries, actual procurement volumes will still depend on subsequent hospital tendering and regulatory registration schedules.

## Beijing Lodges Serious Concerns Over German and Portuguese 5G Vendor Restrictions

Alongside expanding healthcare trade, regulatory friction over European telecommunications infrastructure remains acute. Responding to reports that Germany is revising regulations to classify 5G base stations as critical network components—laying legal groundwork to exclude Chinese equipment and bar Chinese components from future 6G networks—and that Portugal maintained Huawei's 'high-risk supplier' designation following a review under new cybersecurity legislation, He Yadong stated at the [November 12 briefing](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/xwfbzt/2026/swbzklxxwfbh2026n11y12r/index.html) that Beijing has lodged serious concerns with the relevant governments[^1].

MOFCOM emphasized that 5G networks constitute foundational infrastructure for the global digital economy whose deployment and operation should respect market principles, technological neutrality, and international trade rules[^1]. Warning against politicizing technical standards or overstretching national security concepts, the ministry urged European capitals to rectify discriminatory restrictions and maintain an open, predictable business environment—underscoring how sector-specific commercial alignment in medical equipment now coexists with tightening security screening in European network hardware.

## US-China '$30B for $30B' Reciprocal Tariff Talks Progress as NDRC Lowers Domestic Fuel Costs

Addressing China-US economic relations following the November 6 video call between Vice Premier He Lifeng and US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and US Trade Representative Jamieson Greer ahead of next week's APEC summit, He Yadong noted in the [MOFCOM transcript](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/xwfbzt/2026/swbzklxxwfbh2026n11y12r/index.html) that the two countries have conducted eight rounds of economic and trade consultations since May 2025 and that China has implemented prior consensus commitments[^1]. On the proposed '$30 billion for $30 billion' reciprocal tariff reduction framework, He stated that business communities in both countries hold high expectations and that negotiating teams remain in close communication on tariff implementation, bilateral investment, and agricultural trade conditions[^1].

On the domestic cost front, the [NDRC Pricing Department announcement](https://www.ndrc.gov.cn/xwdt/xwfb/202611/t20261112_1408795.html) confirmed that softening international crude oil prices over the preceding 10-working-day pricing window triggered reductions of RMB 645 per ton for standard gasoline and RMB 625 per ton for standard diesel starting at 24:00 on November 12, compared with the previous adjustment on October 29[^2]. State refiners PetroChina, Sinopec, and CNOOC were instructed to ensure stable fuel supply and strict price compliance while local regulators monitor retail enforcement via the national 12315 consumer platform, easing domestic freight expenses during peak fourth-quarter distribution[^2].
