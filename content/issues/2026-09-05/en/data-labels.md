## Starting point

Numbers in a chart need a defined scope. Update dates, units and missing values matter more than visual decoration.

This observation starts with a specific question rather than a universal answer. Experiences vary by place, time and participant. Those differences should be recorded, not collapsed into a general conclusion from a single visit.
