On November 16, China's National Bureau of Statistics (NBS) released its October macroeconomic releases in Beijing, with spokespersons Fu Linghui and Wang Guanhua detailing in the [official October national economic performance press briefing](https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfbhjd/202611/t20261116_1965869.html) that value-added industrial output rose 4.8% year-on-year, the services production index climbed 4.6%, goods trade expanded 31.2%, and the urban surveyed unemployment rate edged down 0.1 percentage points to 5.1%[^1]. Companion statistical bulletins on [October industrial production above designated size](https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfbhjd/202611/t20261116_1965842.html) and [January-October national fixed-asset investment](https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202611/t20261116_1965841.html) underscored sharp structural divergence as semiconductor, robotics, and R&D capital spending outpaced traditional construction[^2][^3].

## Electronics, Robotics, and Integrated Circuits Propel Industrial Value-Added Expansion

According to the [NBS October industrial output statistical release](https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfbhjd/202611/t20261116_1965842.html), real industrial value-added above designated size grew 4.8% year-on-year and 0.21% month-on-month in October, bringing January-October cumulative growth to 5.3%[^2]. Manufacturing output rose 5.4% in October alongside 4.4% growth in utilities and 0.7% in mining, with 28 of 41 major industrial categories posting year-on-year gains—led by a 19.4% surge in computer, communications, and electronic equipment manufacturing, 11.5% in special-purpose machinery, and 9.2% in rail, shipbuilding, and aerospace transport equipment[^2].

Advanced and green hardware lines recorded rapid physical volume gains during the month. High-tech manufacturing value-added accelerated to 17.6% year-on-year growth in October and digital product manufacturing expanded 18.4%, while monthly integrated circuit output climbed 21.9% to 53.2 billion units, industrial robots jumped 26.0% to 82,846 sets, and new energy vehicles rose 17.1% to 2.027 million units[^1][^2]. Over the first ten months, equipment manufacturing grew 10.2% year-on-year to account for 37.9% of total industrial output, while October industrial export delivery value reached 1,558.0 billion yuan, up 18.6% nominally[^1][^2].

## Equipment Purchases and Intellectual Property Capital Spending Counter Headline Investment Contraction

Capital formation data highlighted an ongoing shift from civil construction toward technology and machinery upgrading. As reported in the [January-October fixed-asset investment bulletin](https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202611/t20261116_1965841.html), total fixed-asset investment excluding rural households stood at 36,482.4 billion yuan over the ten-month period, down 8.1% year-on-year on a comparable basis as construction and installation engineering dropped 11.0% and private fixed-asset investment declined 11.4%[^3]. By contrast, spending on equipment, tools, and instruments rose 8.9% year-on-year—contributing 1.5 percentage points to overall investment—while intellectual property product investment grew 9.2%, lifting its share of total investment by 2.5 percentage points[^1][^3].

Sectoral breakdowns show capital concentrating in digital connectivity and high-value manufacturing chains. Within infrastructure, information transmission investment jumped 32.0% year-on-year in January-October and internet and related services surged 48.4%, accompanied by 12.5% growth in both air and water transport[^1][^3]. Across manufacturing subsectors, investment expanded 14.1% in rail, shipbuilding, and aerospace equipment and 9.9% in electronics and communications manufacturing, with electronic circuit manufacturing, integrated circuit fabrication, and lithium-ion battery manufacturing recording year-on-year investment increases of 59.6%, 13.8%, and 18%, respectively[^1][^3].

## AI Hardware Demand and Global Energy Costs Lift PPI to 5.7% Amid Resilient Two-Way Trade

Addressing price dynamics in the [NBS spokesperson Q&A transcript](https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfbhjd/202611/t20261116_1965869.html), Wang Guanhua noted that October consumer prices rose 0.7% year-on-year—with core CPI excluding food and energy up 0.8%—while the Producer Price Index (PPI) climbed 5.7% year-on-year, widening by 0.7 percentage points from September[^1]. Imported international oil and gas volatility combined with winter coal and gas restocking contributed roughly 3.63 percentage points to year-on-year PPI growth, while rapid artificial intelligence deployment lifted factory-gate prices in electrical machinery (+6.2%) and electronics (+5.9%) for a combined 1.18-percentage-point contribution, and automotive manufacturing prices stabilized month-on-month after three consecutive monthly declines[^1].

On domestic consumption and external trade, cumulative retail sales of goods and services increased 2.5% year-on-year in January-October, anchored by 4.9% growth in service retail sales, a 16.5% rise in communications equipment at enterprises above designated size, and wearable smart device sales growth exceeding 70% despite Mid-Autumn Festival calendar shifts weighing on October merchandise growth[^1]. Total goods imports and exports expanded 31.2% year-on-year in October—marking seven straight months of double-digit expansion—with imports up 34.7% and exports of high-tech and green transition products surging past 50%[^1].
