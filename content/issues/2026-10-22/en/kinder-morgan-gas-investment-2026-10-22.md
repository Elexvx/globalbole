Kinder Morgan’s October 21 release puts its shares of Southeast Connector and Permian Link at $3.1 billion and $1.6 billion. Both reached final investment decisions; phased service for the former is targeted from Q4 2030 and the latter for Q3 2030, subject to permits.[^1] Their $4.7 billion sum is the company’s share, not the ventures’ total cost or cash already paid that day.

## More demand does not automatically mean deliverable fuel

Reuters reported designed capacities of 2.6 and 2.8 billion cubic feet daily respectively; Permian Link’s new generation demand is connected to a large data-centre customer.[^2] Computing growth can therefore travel through power systems into fuel transport, but not all pipeline capacity can be labelled AI demand.

The US Energy Information Administration explains that gas reaches users through gathering, processing, transmission and distribution, with compression maintaining transport pressure.[^3] Available production does not guarantee spare transport capacity at a particular destination. Nor does an existing power station establish that its fuel chain is unconstrained at peak times. The location of a constraint matters: production, pipelines and electricity networks are not interchangeable.

Commercially, building a pipeline purchases transport capability over a long horizon. If data centres, power stations and fuel infrastructure arrive on different schedules, one link may be ready before it can be fully used. This is an analysis of coordination risk, not an assertion that these projects have already been delayed.

## A final investment decision is not final delivery

Permian Link’s earlier non-binding solicitation distinguished expressions of interest from definitive agreements and required creditworthiness or acceptable credit support.[^4] Demand discussions, contracts, investment approval and physical transportation are separate stages. Capacity or indicative pricing in an earlier solicitation must not be presented as final contractual terms.

FERC reviews applications to construct and operate interstate gas pipelines.[^5] Company budget approval addresses investment authorisation; it does not replace regulatory permission, land arrangements or construction execution. Even with committed customers, changes to routes, materials or construction costs can alter funding needs and the timing of shareholder returns.

The 2026 decision and anticipated 2030 service belong in different columns: one is a disclosed corporate action, the other a conditional target. Treating future capacity as current supply would exaggerate the investment’s ability to ease immediate electricity constraints.

## What long-term contracts can and cannot protect

For long-lived infrastructure, capacity revenue is different from daily throughput. Where customers undertake to pay for reserved transportation, income need not move one-for-one with daily use. But counterparty credit, contract length, termination terms and continuing payment capacity determine the strength of that protection.

Shareholders must also separate the whole project budget, the company’s ownership share and actual cash expenditure. Joint ventures distribute funding requirements; they do not make overruns, financing costs or maintenance responsibilities disappear. Returns and investment should be measured on matching ownership bases. An apparently attractive multiple may otherwise reflect a denominator that excludes partners’ capital or other costs rather than genuinely superior economics.

## AI infrastructure extends beyond chips

The significance is that digital demand is translating into a slower, heavier construction chain. Servers can be replaced on different cycles; pipelines must traverse permitting, construction and long operation. Energy-transition consequences also require more than labels such as “AI support” or “natural gas”: fuel supply, alternatives, actual load and operating patterns remain relevant.

The next useful evidence is whether customer commitments match the final project scope, key permits arrive on schedule and budgets and service dates remain stable. The commitment demonstrates willingness to allocate capital. Those later steps determine whether it becomes durable service and returns.
