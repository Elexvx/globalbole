## Key observations

Libraries can support reading, learning and community exchange. Visitors have different needs around noise, opening hours and assistance, and the design should respond to those differences.

## How to verify

Service reviews can combine lending records, activity feedback and accessibility experiences. A simple survey should not ask for personal details that are unnecessary for its purpose.
