## Key observations

After submitting a form, users should know whether it succeeded, what happens next and where to check progress. Specific error messages are more useful than a vague failure notice.

## How to verify

Multilingual service involves dates, longer text and assistive technology as well as translated buttons. Tests should include refreshing, going back, keyboard input and narrow screens.

### Follow-up notes

- Record sources and update dates
- Separate facts, plans and observations
