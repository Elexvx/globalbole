## Shenzhen APEC CEO Summit Advances FTAAP Integration and Proposes World Bay Area Economic Cooperation Alliance

According to the [keynote text released by MOFCOM](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/syxwfb/art/2026/art_22eeb85877ae44faad288c66a207a257.html), the Shenzhen summit marks the 25th anniversary of both China's accession to the World Trade Organization (WTO) and its first time hosting APEC. Addressing business leaders in the opening year of China's 15th Five-Year Plan, the speech reported that China's gross domestic product (GDP) expanded by 4.6 percent year-on-year across the first three quarters of 2026, maintaining roughly a 30 percent contribution share to global economic growth. Building on the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area, China formally proposed establishing a World Bay Area Economic Cooperation Alliance to connect coastal innovation and logistics hubs worldwide[^1].

On trade policy and supply-chain governance, the [Shenzhen APEC address](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/syxwfb/art/2026/art_22eeb85877ae44faad288c66a207a257.html) outlined four priority tracks: defending the WTO-centered multilateral trading system while advancing the Free Trade Area of the Asia-Pacific (FTAAP) through convergence between the Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership (RCEP) and the Comprehensive and Progressive Agreement for Trans-Pacific Partnership (CPTPP); expanding digital trade, green trade, and Smart Customs interoperability; maintaining zero foreign-investment restrictions in manufacturing while broadening institutional opening across telecommunications, healthcare, education, and culture; and empowering micro, small, and medium-sized enterprises (MSMEs) through artificial intelligence[^1].

## EU General Affairs Council Tackles Omnibus Regulatory Simplification, MFF Tech Neutrality, and Montenegro Accession

In Brussels, the [Council of the European Union's media advisory for November 19](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/11/18/media-advisory-general-affairs-council-and-accession-conference-with-montenegro-of-19-november-2026/) confirmed that the General Affairs Council will be chaired by Thomas Byrne, Ireland's Minister of State for European Affairs and Defence, representing the rotating Council presidency. Opening in public session at 09:30 CET, ministers will first examine the EU's omnibus simplification legislative packages aimed at cutting administrative burdens for European enterprises, followed by a policy debate on embedding technological neutrality into the next Multiannual Financial Framework (MFF), the Union's long-term budget[^2].

Beyond regulatory simplification and long-term fiscal architecture, the [EU Council agenda](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/11/18/media-advisory-general-affairs-council-and-accession-conference-with-montenegro-of-19-november-2026/) schedules the annual rule-of-law dialogue focusing on country-specific developments in Hungary, Lithuania, Luxembourg, and Cyprus, alongside preparations for the December European Council summit. Over a working lunch, ministers will confer with European Commission Executive Vice-President Maroš Šefčovič on the European Preparedness Union Strategy, before convening at 15:30 CET for the 31st Accession Conference with Montenegro to advance Western Balkan enlargement negotiations[^2].
