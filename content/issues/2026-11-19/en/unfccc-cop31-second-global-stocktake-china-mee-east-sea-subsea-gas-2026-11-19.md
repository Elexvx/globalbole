On November 18, 2026, the [United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (UNFCCC) Secretariat](https://unfccc.int/news/an-ambitious-outcome-is-within-reach-un-climate-chief-at-cop31-event-to-launch-the-second-global) formally launched the two-year Second Global Stocktake (GST2) under the Paris Agreement during a high-level event at the COP31 climate summit in Antalya, Türkiye. On the same day, the [Ministry of Ecology and Environment of the People's Republic of China (MEE)](https://www.mee.gov.cn/ywdt/gsgg/gongshi/gsq/202611/t20261118_1186940.shtml) opened its formal pre-approval environmental impact assessment (EIA) review for CNOOC China Limited's XHZS development in the East China Sea, detailing a new 459-kilometer subsea natural gas trunkline, a 5-billion-cubic-meter-per-year onshore processing expansion in Ningbo, and dedicated carbon dioxide storage piping[^1][^2].

## COP31 in Antalya Initiates Second Global Stocktake as 90 Percent of Parties Embed First-Stocktake Goals

Speaking at the Antalya launch, [UN Climate Change Executive Secretary Simon Stiell](https://unfccc.int/news/an-ambitious-outcome-is-within-reach-un-climate-chief-at-cop31-event-to-launch-the-second-global) described the Global Stocktake as the central ratchet mechanism of the Paris Agreement, noting that the second cycle beginning at COP31 will run for two years before concluding at COP33. Stiell reported that the first Global Stocktake has already reshaped national energy planning: fully 90 percent of Parties submitting updated Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs) explicitly incorporated the first stocktake's headline energy and adaptation mandates[^1].

According to the [UNFCCC assessment](https://unfccc.int/news/an-ambitious-outcome-is-within-reach-un-climate-chief-at-cop31-event-to-launch-the-second-global), those national commitments reflect the global pledge to triple renewable power generation capacity and double the annual rate of energy efficiency improvements by 2030, alongside commitments to transition away from fossil fuels and implement adaptation measures under the UAE Framework for Global Climate Resilience. Stiell stressed that the Second Global Stocktake will rely heavily on updated scientific inputs from the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC) while pressing for verifiable finance, technology transfer, and capacity building for developing economies[^1].

## China Reviews 459-Kilometer East China Sea Gas Trunkline With Marine Spawning Closures and CO2 Tank Farm Routing

At the infrastructure implementation level, [China's Ministry of Ecology and Environment public review notice](https://www.mee.gov.cn/ywdt/gsgg/gongshi/gsq/202611/t20261118_1186940.shtml)—running for five working days from November 18 to November 24, 2026, on the EIA compiled by CNOOC Research Institute—illustrates how major offshore gas supply projects are being bound by stricter carbon-handling and marine biodiversity mandates. Located in the East China Sea and Ningbo, Zhejiang Province, the XHZS project comprises seven new offshore platforms, four multiphase infield pipelines, a 23.2-kilometer subsea oil pipeline connecting the HG CEP platform to the ZZ CEPA platform, and a 459-kilometer subsea natural gas trunkline running from HG CEP directly to the Ningbo onshore terminal[^2].

Crucially, the [MEE environmental review dossier](https://www.mee.gov.cn/ywdt/gsgg/gongshi/gsq/202611/t20261118_1186940.shtml) pairs the Ningbo terminal's 5-billion-cubic-meter annual gas processing expansion with a new 225-meter inlet pipeline (including a 150-meter horizontal directional drilling crossing) and a 75-meter pipe rack routed directly to an onshore carbon dioxide (CO2) tank farm to capture and isolate separated CO2 streams. To protect marine ecosystems under national standards GB 4914-2008 and GB 18420.1-2009, the EIA mandates that all non-compliant oil-based drill cuttings and fluids be shipped ashore for disposal, while subsea trenching is strictly prohibited during the special protection window of the Xiangshan Port Spanish Mackerel National Germplasm Reserve (March 1 to July 31), inside the core zone of the East China Sea Hairtail Reserve (April 16 to July 1), and throughout the May peak spawning season[^2].
